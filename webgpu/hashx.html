HashX on WebGPU

The same 512-instruction program compiled to a WGSL compute shader and to WebAssembly, in one browser and one process. The page does not trust the GPU: before using it, it hashes eight known nonces and compares the digests against the WebAssembly module. If they disagree — which is what the Intel Vulkan backend on this laptop does — the page reports it and falls back to WebAssembly instead of returning wrong answers faster.
