Does the Intel HashX miscompile depend on the dispatch shape?

One shader, one nonce, several dispatch sizes. A nonce is computed with 1, 2…8 active invocations in the workgroup, and once more with the nonce sitting at invocation index n instead of 0. If any of those disagree with each other, the wrong answer is not a property of the arithmetic alone — it depends on how the work was scheduled. If they all agree and all differ from WebAssembly, it is arithmetic-level and a smaller reproducer can be extracted from the shader.
